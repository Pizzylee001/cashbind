CashBind The Story of CashBind 02 / 06

Why Nostr Needs a Cryptographic BCH Bridge

The Gap Today

1. Fragile Payment Trust

Merchants and creators on Nostr hold portable npub identities, yet accepting BCH still forces payers to trust bio screenshots or centralized handle directories.

2. Registrar & Impersonation Risk

Centralized payment handles can be hijacked, censored, or silently swapped without cryptographic proof of ownership.

3. Dangerous Key Reuse

Reusing a Bitcoin Cash custody key inside social Nostr clients puts real mainnet funds at risk if any social app leaks keys.

The CashBind Solution

1. Dual-Key Cross-Signing

A BCH secp256k1 key (ECDSA) and a distinct Nostr key (BIP-340 Schnorr) both sign the exact same canonical JSON binding payload.

2. Immutable BCH Mainnet Anchor

A compact 46-byte OP_RETURN CASHBIND v1 <sha256> output anchors the digest permanently on BCH mainnet for under $0.01.

3. Zero-Trust Verify Before Pay

Any wallet or dApp resolves npub -> verified CashAddress directly from public Nostr relays and Fulcrum nodes with zero middlemen.

CashBind Benefits & Cryptographic Safeguards 03 / 06

Zero-Trust Protection Built Into Every Step

Strict Key Separation

Protocol rejects any binding where BCH and Nostr keys share the same secp256k1 x-coordinate.

1-Byte Tamper Lock

Mutating even one character breaks the Dual-Key Notary Seal and locks BCH mainnet payment execution.

Zero Central Database

Proofs publish directly to 3 independent public Nostr relays and verify completely offline.

Stateless Challenge Auth

Expiring nonces authenticate users via bound Nostr (NIP-42) or BCH signatures with no passwords.

Key Rotation & W3C DID

Unbroken prevBindingHash rotation chains, third-party Schnorr attestations, and did:nostr export.

CashBind BCH Mainnet Usage & WizardConnect 04 / 06

Real Bitcoin Cash Mainnet Transactions, Non-Custodially

Native WizardConnect Pairing

Pairs via QR/URI with Paytaca, Selene, and Cashonize to sign OP_RETURN anchors and direct payments without exposing private keys.

46-Byte OP_RETURN Commitment

Builds real SIGHASH_ALL | SIGHASH_FORKID transactions enforcing the 546-satoshi dust limit and dynamic miner fees.

Direct Verified BCH Payments

Resolves any Nostr identity to its verified CashAddress, generates BIP-21 URIs, and broadcasts mainnet payouts with Blockchair links.

Multi-Node Fulcrum Failover

Serverless /api/bch/* proxy fails over across bch.imaginary.cash, bch.loping.net, and fulcrum.greyh.at.
